What counts as a translation

Only a translation you asked for and actually got back. Re-selecting text you already translated today is free, a failed request costs nothing, and switching the target language on a result you have already seen does not charge you twice. The count resets at your own midnight, not at UTC.

How you pay

In Turkey the checkout is PayTR and you are charged in lira, for a fixed term that does not renew by itself. Everywhere else it is Paddle, as a subscription you can cancel at any time. The checkout picks the right one for you.

Before you buy

What happens when I run out?

The bubble tells you the allowance is spent and offers Pro. Nothing else changes, and the count comes back at midnight.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up and no password. A purchase gives you a licence key, and the key is all the app ever asks for.

How many machines?

Three at a time, on one key. Freeing a slot takes one click in the app on a machine you no longer use.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days, no reason needed. Cancelling is separate: you keep Pro until the period you paid for ends.
