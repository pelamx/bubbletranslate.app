Frequently asked questions

Everything worth knowing before you install.

Is BubbleTranslate free?

Yes. The builds on this page are free to download and use, with no trial and no translation limit. No account or sign-up is needed.

Which systems does it run on?

macOS and Linux. There's no Windows build yet. On Linux you need glibc 2.43 or newer for the prebuilt binary; on an older distribution, build from source.

Does it need an internet connection?

Yes. Translations come from online providers — Google, MyMemory, and optionally DeepL — tried in order until one answers. Only the text you select is sent, and only to translate it.

What permission does it need?

On macOS, Accessibility — that's how the app sees what you've selected. It's the only permission it asks for. On Linux nothing extra is required on most X11 and Wayland sessions.

How do I get the best translation quality?

Add a free DeepL API key in the app's Providers section. DeepL gives the best results where it supports the language; without a key the app uses Google and MyMemory, which are free and need no setup.

Where does it live while I work?

In the macOS menu bar or the Linux tray, as a small globe. Closing the window doesn't quit the app — it keeps watching your selections. Quit from the icon's menu.
