2. What the app does

BubbleTranslate runs in the background on macOS and Linux. When you select text in another application, a small bubble appears near your cursor showing a translation of it. The app detects the source language on its own and translates into the language you have chosen.

The app does not translate anything itself. It sends your selected text to a third-party translation service — Google Translate, MyMemory or DeepL, in the order you configure — and displays what comes back. Section 7 covers what that means for you.
